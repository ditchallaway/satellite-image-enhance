# Safety Checklist

Before delivering enhanced satellite imagery, verify:

1. The output still represents the same geographic scene and extent.
2. Terrain, coastlines, water bodies, land cover, roads, and infrastructure did not change materially.
3. No fabricated terrain features, invented land cover, or false geographic detail was added.
4. Crucial topological or environmental data was not hidden or obscured.
5. Cloud-covered areas were not filled with invented detail.
6. Spectral band relationships were not distorted beyond user-specified visualization.
7. Batch outputs have consistent color, contrast, and geographic presentation.
8. The user has reviewed or will review the files before public or operational use.
