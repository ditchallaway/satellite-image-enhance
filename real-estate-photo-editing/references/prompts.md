# Prompt Patterns

## General Listing Cleanup

```text
Improve this real-estate listing photo lightly and truthfully. Make it cleaner, brighter, and more balanced while preserving the same room, geometry, finishes, window framing, fixtures, appliances, furniture layout, and truthful property appearance. Do not redesign, restage, invent sunlight, change exterior views, or hide material defects.
```

## Remove Only

```text
Remove only the specified clutter. Change nothing else. Preserve room geometry, materials, window framing, fixtures, furniture layout, lighting direction, and truthful listing appearance. Do not redesign, restage, invent objects, or alter fixed property features.
```

## Kitchen

```text
Clean the temporary loose items and lightly improve brightness and white balance. Keep cabinets, countertops, backsplash, appliances, handles, seams, and reflections truthful and unchanged.
```

## Bathroom

```text
Make the bathroom look clean and bright but realistic. Preserve tile lines, mirror shape, vanity, fixtures, shower edges, and true room geometry. Do not over-whiten or turn surfaces into plastic.
```

## Window Handling

```text
Balance the window area naturally. Do not invent a skyline, greenery, sunlight direction, or exterior details. If the original view is unclear, keep the window neutral and believable.
```
